# Bank of America (United States)

- Transactions are grouped into sections: "Deposits and other additions" and "Withdrawals and
  other subtractions" (with further groups for checks and service fees on some accounts). Amounts
  can be printed without a sign, so the section decides it.
- Each section ends with a "Total ..." line. These totals match the account summary and are not
  transactions.
- Dates print as `MM/DD/YY`.
- The account summary gives the beginning balance, the total of each section, and the ending
  balance.
- The section heading repeats as "... - continued" on the next page.

See also: [Common gotchas](../gotchas.md), [Checking a conversion](../checking.md).
